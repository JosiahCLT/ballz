How it works
Goal: survive longer than your opponent.
  • You aim + shoot your balls each round.
  • Blocks lose 1 HP per hit until they break.
  • Pick up +1 / +2 / +3 to increase your ball count.
  • After each round, blocks move down. If they hit the red line → you lose.
  • You have a turn timer. If it runs out, the game auto-shoots.
